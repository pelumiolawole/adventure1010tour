Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: June 2026. Governing law: Federal Republic of Nigeria.

This Privacy Policy explains how Adventure 101 Tour Limited (BN 3268546, "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and handles personal information when you contact us, make an enquiry, or use our services. We take your privacy seriously and handle your data with care.

If you have questions, contact us at info@adventure101.tours or WhatsApp +234 909 864 0296.

1. What Personal Data We Collect

1.1 Contact Form

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Service interest (e.g. tours, private jet, bespoke vacation)
  • The content of your message or enquiry

1.2 WhatsApp and Direct Communication

When you contact us via WhatsApp, email, or social media direct message, we receive the contact information associated with your account and the content of your messages. We use this solely to respond to your enquiry and manage your booking.

1.3 Booking Information

For confirmed bookings, we collect additional information needed to arrange your travel: passport details, travel dates, dietary requirements, emergency contact information, and payment records. This information is collected directly from you during the booking process.

1.4 What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect payment card numbers directly. Payments are made via bank transfer or approved payment links. We do not use tracking pixels, advertising cookies, or behavioural analytics tools on this website.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the personal data you provide for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry
  • To arrange and manage your booking
  • To communicate updates about your trip (itinerary changes, payment reminders, pre-departure information)
  • To send confirmations, receipts, and booking documents
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use your data for automated profiling or decision-making.

3. EmailJS: Contact Form Data Processor

Our contact form uses EmailJS (emailjs.com) to transmit your enquiry to our team. When you submit the form, your data passes through EmailJS's servers before reaching us. EmailJS acts as a data processor on our behalf.

EmailJS stores form submissions temporarily to deliver them. We recommend reviewing EmailJS's privacy policy if you want details on their data handling practices.

If you prefer not to use the contact form, you can reach us directly via WhatsApp at +234 909 864 0296 or by email at info@adventure101.tours.

4. WhatsApp as a Communication Channel

We use WhatsApp Business to communicate with clients. When you message us on WhatsApp, your messages and phone number are visible to our team. WhatsApp is operated by Meta Platforms Inc. and is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

We do not share the content of your WhatsApp messages with third parties, except where required by law.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep enquiry data for as long as it is relevant to managing your relationship with us. If you enquired but did not book, we may retain your contact details for up to 12 months in case you return with further questions.

For confirmed bookings, we retain booking records, payment records, and trip documentation for a minimum of 6 years to comply with Nigerian financial and business record-keeping requirements.

You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Who Can Access Your Data

Access to your personal data is limited to Adventure 101 Tour Limited staff and contractors who need it to service your booking. We do not give third-party suppliers (hotels, airlines, transport operators) more information than they need to fulfil your booking.

We may disclose your data if required to do so by Nigerian law, court order, or government authority.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Withdraw consent to receive marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email info@adventure101.tours with the subject line "Privacy Request". We will respond within 10 business days.

8. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or any tracking scripts. No cookie consent banner is displayed because there is nothing to consent to.

If this changes in the future (for example, if we add analytics), this policy will be updated and a cookie notice will be added to the site.

9. External Links

This website contains links to external platforms including Instagram, X (Twitter), and WhatsApp. Once you leave our site, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18 without parental consent. If you are booking on behalf of a minor, you confirm you have the authority to share their information with us and accept our terms on their behalf.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be communicated to active clients by email or WhatsApp. Continued use of our services after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any disputes relating to this policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Nigerian courts.
