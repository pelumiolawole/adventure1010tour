# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Nigerian professionals and diaspora (UK-based and home) aged 28-50 who want premium, curated travel without the chaos of planning it themselves. They travel for meaningful experiences: East Africa safaris, West African road trips, island getaways. They are aspirational, quality-conscious, and willing to pay for reliability and white-glove service. Secondary users: corporate clients seeking private jet charter and birth tourism clients seeking Barbados.

## Product Purpose

Adventure 101 Tour Limited is a Nigerian premium travel company that plans and executes bespoke group and private tours across Africa, the Middle East, and beyond. The website's job is to convert a visitor from "curious" to "WhatsApp inquiry sent" — building enough trust and desire that they reach out. Success means an enquiry, not a booking form completed.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, warm, grounded. Not flashy or hyperactive. The brand feels like a trusted friend who happens to know every hidden restaurant in Accra and can charter a jet to Zanzibar. Three words: premium, unhurried, African.

## Anti-references

- Generic OTA/booking-engine UX (Expedia, Booking.com grids)
- Bland corporate travel (grey-and-blue palettes, stock photo lifestyle shots)
- Overhyped adventure-sport aesthetics (neon, extreme sport energy)
- Western safari luxury clichés (colonial palette, safari-khaki color schemes)
- Cramped mobile-first app UI patterns (this is a brand surface, not a product)

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before desire** — copy and structure earn credibility first; emotional desire follows. Never lead with hype.
2. **Destination, not decoration** — every visual choice should make you feel closer to the place, not impress you with the design.
3. **Premium through restraint** — fewer elements done better. White space, unhurried pacing, and confident typography signal quality better than busy layouts.
4. **Warm authority** — amber (#A86704) and dark backgrounds carry the brand's warmth; typography and layout carry its authority.
5. **One clear action** — every page should funnel to a single WhatsApp or enquiry CTA. Never compete with yourself.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Dark-mode only (no light mode). Body text must hit ≥4.5:1 on the dark background (#0e0d0a). Reduced motion media query required on all animations. Focus states visible for keyboard navigation. Touch targets minimum 44px.
